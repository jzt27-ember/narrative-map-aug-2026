# Thailand Energy Narratives — Source Extract
*Compiled August 24, 2026*

Sources: Bangkok Post, The Nation Thailand, IEA, ACE (ASEAN Centre for Energy), Bloomberg/BloombergNEF, Carbon Trust, Ember Energy, and secondary trade press.

## Bangkok Post

**[Thailand's energy future includes the sun](https://www.bangkokpost.com/business/general/3306049/thailands-energy-future-includes-the-sun)**
Frames accelerating Thailand's transition from fossil fuels to clean energy as an urgent government priority. Focused on rooftop solar, regulatory commission activity, and incentive schemes. Published mid-August 2026.

**[Officials mull strategies for clean energy](https://www.bangkokpost.com/business/general/3302937/officials-mull-strategies-for-clean-energy)**
The government is weighing four energy management strategies under the new PDP, intended to guide Thailand toward net-zero by 2050 and set long-term electricity prices, according to an anonymous energy official. Published Aug 17, 2026.

**[Clean energy the focus of power plan](https://www.bangkokpost.com/business/general/3276602/clean-energy-the-focus-of-power-plan)**
Energy authorities are preparing a new PDP (2026-2050) designed to boost clean energy and meet surging demand, especially from data centres, guiding Thailand toward net-zero by 2050. Energy Minister Akanat Promphan set a 60% clean-electricity target, and the plan assumes annual GDP growth of 2-2.5% with electricity tariffs of 4 baht/kWh. Published June 26, 2026.

**[Climate pressure spurs Thailand's energy revamp](https://www.bangkokpost.com/business/general/3212863/climate-pressure-spurs-thailands-energy-revamp)**
The draft PDP seeks to accelerate renewables and introduce nuclear power (SMRs), framed as vital to Thailand's emissions pledge reaffirmed at the November 2025 UN climate summit in Brazil. Notes SMR capacity debates and Thailand's NDC 3.0. Published March 9, 2026.

**[Bright but patchy outlook for solar push](https://www.bangkokpost.com/business/general/3249792/bright-but-patchy-outlook-for-solar-push)**
Analysts urge a pivot to solar plus battery storage amid an energy crisis; the Israel-US war on Iran disrupted supply chains, echoing the 2022 Russia-Ukraine shock when costs surged 19% (~130 billion baht), with the current crisis described as more severe. Covers the personal income tax deduction (up to 200,000 baht) for rooftop solar installed March 2026–Dec 2027. Published May 6, 2026.

**[Official: Solar power can lead energy transition](https://www.bangkokpost.com/business/general/3260428/official-solar-power-can-lead-energy-transition)**
Discusses declining BESS costs enabling solar-led transition, noting traditional solar's ~10% capacity factor limitation. Reiterates the rooftop tax incentive scheme. Published May 25, 2026.

**[Thailand backs energy drive](https://www.bangkokpost.com/thailand/general/3237403/thailand-backs-energy-drive)**
Thailand endorsed "Azec 2.0" at the Asia Zero Emission Community summit, with PM Anutin Charnvirakul joining regional leaders including Japan's PM, alongside IEA and Asian Development Bank representatives. Published ~April 2026.

**[Energy wake-up call](https://www.bangkokpost.com/business/general/3226873/energy-wakeup-call)**
Calls the 2026 PDP a turning point requiring balance between supply stability, tariffs, and clean fuels; past PDPs over-relied on fossil imports, leaving Thailand exposed to geopolitical shocks. Also covers EV fleet transitions at Thailand Post and Grab Thailand. Published ~March 2026.

## The Nation Thailand

**[Renewable & Green Energy section](https://www.nationthailand.com/category/sustainability/renewable-green-energy)**
Aggregator page — covers Bangchak's first commercial SAF (sustainable aviation fuel) production, EGAT's plan to double renewable share via floating solar and SMR/hydrogen tech, and biodiversity-linked green investment framing.

**[Thailand Faces 'Worst Energy Crisis in History' as Diesel Prices Treble](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/policy/40064638)**
Energy permanent secretary Prasert Sinsukprasert said global diesel prices hit nearly $300/barrel (triple the $92 standard) as of April 3, 2026 — worse than the 2022 Ukraine-driven peak of $150 — with the state Fuel Fund nearly 50 billion baht in deficit.

**[Thailand Renewable Energy Pipeline Draws Up to US$2.61bn in Investment](https://www.nationthailand.com/business/economy/40069862)**
The ERC has licensed 449 renewable projects (1,607.58 MW) since 2024, expected to draw $2.10-2.61 billion in investment; solar makes up the largest share at ~400 projects.

**[Waste-to-Energy Drives Thailand's Clean Power Shift](https://www.nationthailand.com/pr-news/sustaination/40067430)**
Details the PDP 2026 draft extending the planning horizon to 25 years and moving the Net Zero target forward from 2065 to 2050, with clean energy targeted at 51-60% of the power mix by 2037. Also covers a rural biomass innovation (BioCycle Kiln) in Chiang Rai.

**[Thailand Prepares Gastech 2026 as a Gateway to Global Energy Security](https://www.nationthailand.com/blogs/business/economy/40070071)**
As Gastech 2026 host, Thailand will showcase LNG infrastructure and Andaman Sea petroleum potential; the event expects 50,000+ participants and ~10 billion baht in related economic activity. Agenda centers on Andaman investment, ASEAN LNG hub ambitions, and Direct PPAs.

**[Thailand pushes Land Bridge as energy hub to guard against Hormuz risk](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/policy/40065798)**
The Land Bridge project includes oil storage facilities and a cross-coastline pipeline, positioning Thailand as an alternative to Singapore for Middle Eastern crude storage/trade and mitigating Strait of Hormuz closure risk. Includes plans for a state-owned Strategic Petroleum Reserve.

**[Thailand outlines energy transition towards Net Zero economy](https://www.nationthailand.com/business/economy/40067871)**
Energy Minister Akanat Promphan said energy-transition discussions often wrongly focus only on electricity, when oil and (increasingly) data centres/AI also drive national energy strategy simultaneously.

**[Commerce lifts 2026 inflation forecast as energy prices start to bite](https://www.nationthailand.com/business/economy/40064758)**
Thailand's Commerce Ministry raised its 2026 inflation forecast to 1.5-2.5% (from 0.0-1.0%), driven by rising global crude prices tied to geopolitical tensions, despite March marking a 12th straight month of deflation.

**[Energy Minister unveils power plan to support data centre investment](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/policy/40058825)**
Covers Minister Auttapol Rerkpiboon's remarks on Thailand's strategy to attract data centre investment amid AI-driven demand growth. Published Nov 2025.

## IEA

**[Southeast Asia Energy Outlook 2026](https://www.iea.org/reports/southeast-asia-energy-outlook-2026)**
Seventh edition of this IEA regional outlook, covering all 11 ASEAN states; this year's focus is the region's pre-Middle East-conflict trajectory and how the energy crisis triggered by that conflict affects policy and investment priorities.

**[Southeast Asia Energy Outlook 2026 — Executive Summary](https://www.iea.org/reports/southeast-asia-energy-outlook-2026/executive-summary)**
Coal supplies roughly half of regional electricity; Indonesia dominates production (~90%), with Vietnam, Thailand and the Philippines producing smaller domestic amounts. Coal use is linked to an estimated 330,000 premature deaths in 2024 from air pollution.

**[Southeast Asia Energy Outlook 2026 — Full PDF](https://iea.blob.core.windows.net/assets/b99d4ce3-3ed4-4d82-b274-bbedd46146b8/SoutheastAsiaEnergyOutlook2026.pdf)**
Notes Thailand is expected to launch a 2 GW direct power purchase scheme; the IEA's Regional Cooperation Centre opened in Singapore in late 2024; Thailand became an IEA Association country alongside Indonesia, Singapore and Vietnam in 2026.

**[Electricity 2026 — Analysis](https://www.iea.org/reports/electricity-2026)**
IEA's annual global electricity report, this year expanding its forecast window to five years (2026-2030) and adding new chapters on grid flexibility, demand response, and utility-scale batteries amid AI/data-centre-driven demand growth.

**[Thailand's Clean Electricity Transition](https://www.iea.org/reports/thailands-clean-electricity-transition)**
A joint IEA-EGAT-Ministry of Energy analysis comparing the PDP's planned trajectory against Thailand's emissions targets, using a PLEXOS model of the Thai power system built with EGAT.

**[Driving global and regional collaboration to realise the ASEAN Power Grid vision](https://www.iea.org/commentaries/driving-global-and-regional-collaboration-to-realise-the-asean-power-grid-vision)**
A commentary arguing the finalized 2026-2030 APAEC and renewed ASEAN Power Grid MOU are a key opportunity to turn longstanding regional grid-integration commitments into reality, requiring closer ACE-ASEC coordination.

## Bloomberg / BloombergNEF

**[Thailand: Turning Point for a Net-Zero Power Grid](https://assets.bbhub.io/professional/sites/44/19-05-2025_Thailand_Turning-Point-for-a-Net-Zero-Power-Grid.pdf)**
As of 2024, gas accounted for ~68% of domestic power generation and coal 17% (out of 201 TWh produced), with Thailand increasingly reliant on LNG imports (29% of gas consumption) as domestic gas output declines. Thailand had not yet submitted its updated NDC as of this report; its prior 2022 NDC targeted a 30% unconditional / 40% conditional emissions cut by 2030 versus business-as-usual.

## Carbon Trust

**[Spurring Thailand's energy transition with a dedicated roadmap](https://www.carbontrust.com/en-la/our-work-and-impact/impact-stories/spurring-thailands-energy-transition-with-a-dedicated-roadmap)**
Commissioned by the UK FCDO to support Thailand's EPPO, identifying core barriers: unclear targets/trajectories, obsolete regulatory and contractual clauses, insufficient grid infrastructure, and market players working in silos. The Carbon Trust engaged 50 local and international experts, including Thailand's ERC and Chulalongkorn University academics; the roadmap has since become part of Thailand's refreshed National Energy Plan.

## Ember Energy

**[Thailand's 2037 power sector targets](https://ember-energy.org/latest-insights/thailands-cost-optimal-pathway-to-a-sustainable-economy/thailands-2037-power-sector-targets/)**
The draft revised PDP (June 2024 hearing) targets 33% renewables by 2030 and 51% by 2037, up from a prior 36% target; it limits net gas capacity additions to 2 GW in 2035-2036 while enabling ~50 GW of new renewables and 14 GW of storage by 2037. Ember's Electricity Review 2026 found solar generation grew 72% to 9 TWh in 2025, reaching 5% of the power mix, despite Thailand having used only about 1% of its solar potential.

## Other Secondary Sources

**[Thailand's New PDP Targets 60% Clean Power by 2050 (Reccessary)](https://www.reccessary.com/en/news/thailand-pdp-2026)**
Frames the PDP finalization against PM Anutin's first 100 days in office, a period shaped by fuel price volatility and pressure to cut import exposure.

**[Three forces set to shape Thailand's renewable energy investment path (Reccessary)](https://www.reccessary.com/en/news/three-forces-shape-thailand-renewable-investment)**
Identifies three 2026 drivers: the delayed PDP, AI's role in accelerating low-carbon transformation, and expanding direct power purchase agreements (DPPAs), against the backdrop of a February 2026 general election.

**[Power Development Plan 2026 Advances Alignment with Net-Zero (Lexology)](https://www.lexology.com/library/detail.aspx?g=a6039ec0-ba05-455c-bcaa-48699d2ae210)**
Legal-analysis summary noting the plan targets over 50% clean generation, emphasizing utility-scale solar, on/offshore wind, and EGAT floating solar, with technical renewable potential assessed at 5,000-10,000 MW.

**[Renewable Energy in Thailand: An Alternative to LNG (Energy Tracker Asia)](https://energytracker.asia/renewable-energy-in-thailand-an-alternative-to-lng/)**
Renewables' share of Thailand's power mix rose from 8% to 15% over the past decade; cumulative solar PV capacity hit 9.9 GWp in 2024 (6.3 GWp ground-mounted, 3.3 GWp rooftop); BNEF's Global Climatescope found clean energy investment reached $1.18 billion in 2024, up 73% from 2023.

**[Thailand — Climate Performance Ranking 2026 (CCPI)](https://ccpi.org/country/tha/)**
Thailand rates medium on GHG emissions/energy use but low on renewable energy and climate policy; experts recommend a complete coal phase-out by 2035 and criticize continued fossil-fuel dependence.

**[Green Cities, Clean Markets: World Bank Backs Thailand's Low-Carbon Transition (FASI)](https://fasi.eu/en/articles/news/30436-green-cities-clean-markets-world-bank-backs-thailand-s-low-carbon-transition.html)**
The World Bank approved a $200 million Low Carbon Cities and Carbon Market Development Project, expected to install up to 180 MW of renewable capacity and save ~448 GWh annually, ahead of Thailand hosting the 2026 IMF-World Bank Annual Meeting.
